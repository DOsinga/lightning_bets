Each bet gets two fresh secrets from the oracle on this page, one for yes and one for no. You and your friend each send your stake as a Lightning payment that is held, not settled, locked to the secret of your friend's side. After full time the oracle reveals the winning secret: the winner uses it to collect, the loser hands the other stake back. Nobody, the oracle included, ever holds the money. Results come from ESPN.

It runs on Mutinynet, a test network with free coins and 30 second blocks, so it's play money. The bet program is a command line tool for Apple Silicon Macs.

Getting set up

  1. Download bet and check it runs. This creates your node in ~/.lightning_bets: 
    curl -o bet {{ download_url }}
chmod +x bet
./bet info
    (Download it with curl rather than your browser, or macOS will refuse to run it.) {% if oracle_url %}
    This copy of the page runs its own oracle, so also do: 
    export BET_ORACLE={{ oracle_url }}
    {% endif %}
  2. Get some coins: paste the fund at address from ./bet info into the Mutinynet faucet. Blocks come every 30 seconds, so a minute later ./bet info shows the balance.
  3. Open a channel with your friend. One of you has to be reachable: on the same network that's easy, otherwise forward port 9735 on your router. That person keeps their node online with 
    ./bet run
    and sends the other their node id and address. The other one opens a channel and pushes half of it across, so you both have something to bet with: 
    ./bet open <node id>@<their ip>:9735 200000 --push 100000
    After a few blocks ./bet info shows the channel as usable. You only do this once; the channel works for every bet after.

Placing a bet

Pick a match. Because the payments are held on the chain's clock, a bet can only be placed up to 11 hours before kickoff, and not after it.

The command prints a bet id. Your friend runs


./bet accept <bet id>


which shows them the question and both stakes before anything is paid. Keep both running until
they say done: the stakes go across before kickoff, and after full time the winner
collects. If you have to stop, ./bet watch <bet id> picks up where it left off,
as long as you're back before the match ends.



The oracle


For anyone who wants to build their own client:


POST /projects/lightning_bets/api/bets
     {"league": "eng.1", "event_id": "...", "outcome": "home", "offerer": "yes",
      "stakes": {"yes": 10000, "no": 10000}}
GET  /projects/lightning_bets/api/bets/<bet_id>
POST /projects/lightning_bets/api/bets/<bet_id>/invoice
     {"side": "yes", "invoice": "lntbs..."}


Bets resolve on the final score as ESPN reports it, extra time included and penalty
shootouts not, and are void if the match is postponed, cancelled or abandoned.
